CVSWHO POLICIES

Terms and Conditions

Please read these Terms and Conditions carefully before creating and using a CVSWHO student account.

Last updated: October 2026

By registering for, accessing, or using CVSWHO, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to these Terms and Conditions.

CVSWHO is a student profile management platform intended to allow students to maintain academic, personal, organizational, accomplishment, and other profile-related information.

01

Acceptance of Terms

You must accept these Terms and Conditions before creating a CVSWHO account. If you do not agree with these terms, you should not create or use an account.

02

Student Account Eligibility

Student registration is intended for eligible Cavite State University students using an official CvSU student email address accepted by the platform.

Administrator accounts are separately created and cannot be created through the public student registration page.

03

Accuracy of Information

You are responsible for providing accurate, current, and truthful information in your CVSWHO account and student profile.

You should not intentionally submit false information, impersonate another student, falsify accomplishments, misrepresent organizational membership, or upload misleading documents.

04

Administrator Access to Student Information

By using CVSWHO, you acknowledge that authorized CVSWHO administrators may access information associated with your account and student profile when reasonably necessary for platform administration, student record management, verification, moderation, security, troubleshooting, account support, and enforcement of platform rules.

This administrative access may include information you provide to CVSWHO such as your name, student information, contact information, academic information, address, family or guardian information, About Me information, accomplishments, hobbies and interests, organizations, uploaded documents, profile settings, privacy settings, account status, and other information stored as part of your CVSWHO record.

Important distinction

Privacy settings such as Public, School Only, and Private control visibility to ordinary visitors and other student users. They do not necessarily prevent authorized administrators from accessing the information for legitimate administrative purposes.

05

Profile Privacy and Visibility

CVSWHO may provide privacy settings that allow profile information to be classified as Public, School Only, or Private.

  • Public information may be visible to visitors without a CVSWHO account.
  • School Only information is intended for authenticated and authorized CVSWHO school users.
  • Private information is hidden from ordinary users but may still be available to authorized administrators when required for legitimate administrative purposes.

Students are responsible for reviewing their privacy settings and selecting the visibility appropriate for information they choose to provide.

06

Account Security

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your password, verification codes, and account access.

You must not knowingly provide your login credentials to another person or attempt to gain unauthorized access to another user's account.

07

Verification

CVSWHO may require email or account verification before an account can be activated or accessed.

Verification codes are intended only for the account holder and should not be shared with other people.

08

Acceptable Use

You agree not to misuse CVSWHO. Prohibited activities include:

  • Attempting to access another person's account without authorization.
  • Uploading malicious, fraudulent, deceptive, or intentionally harmful content.
  • Attempting to bypass authentication, privacy controls, or administrator restrictions.
  • Using CVSWHO to harass, threaten, impersonate, or misrepresent another individual.
  • Attempting to interfere with the operation or security of the platform.
09

Student-Submitted Content

Students remain responsible for information, descriptions, accomplishments, organization records, files, profile photos, and other content that they submit to CVSWHO.

You should only upload content that you are permitted to use and that is appropriate for a student profile management platform.

10

Account Moderation and Status

Authorized administrators may change an account's status when necessary for administrative or security reasons. Depending on the platform's available controls, an account may be active, pending, suspended, archived, deactivated, or placed in Trash.

11

Account Removal and Trash

When an administrator removes a student account, CVSWHO may temporarily place that account in Trash instead of immediately deleting it.

While an account is in Trash, access to the account may be disabled. The account may be restored during the applicable recovery period.

Under the current CVSWHO account-management design, accounts placed in Trash are scheduled for permanent deletion after 30 days unless they are restored before the deletion date.

12

Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained while an account is active and for periods reasonably necessary for account recovery, administration, security, record management, or deletion processing.

Permanent account deletion may also remove related student-profile records where the database and platform are configured to delete those dependent records.

13

Service Availability

CVSWHO may occasionally be unavailable because of maintenance, technical problems, system updates, or other operational requirements.

Continuous or uninterrupted availability is not guaranteed.

14

Changes to the Platform

Features, privacy controls, account-management functions, and other parts of CVSWHO may be updated as the platform develops.

15

Changes to These Terms

These Terms and Conditions may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to CVSWHO's functionality, policies, or administrative procedures.

The date shown at the top of this page should be updated whenever material changes are made.

16

Contact and Questions

Questions regarding these Terms and Conditions or the use of CVSWHO should be directed to the appropriate CVSWHO administrator or authorized project representative.
